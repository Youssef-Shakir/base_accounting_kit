DoniaLink Accounting

محاسبة دنيا لينك

Complete accounting for Odoo 18 Community, made for cafés and restaurants: interactive financial reports you can click, compare and export, plus assets, budgets, post-dated cheques, follow-ups and bank statement import.

Interactive reports

  • Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance, General Ledger, Partner Ledger and Aged Receivable / Payable open as live reports.
  • Filters in the report: this month, quarter, financial year, custom dates, posted or all entries, search.
  • Comparison with the previous period or the same period last year.
  • Clickable lines: open the journal items of an account or the journal entry of a line, and come back with your filters kept.
  • Export what you see to PDF or Excel (XLSX).

التقارير التفاعلية

  • الأرباح والخسائر، الميزانية العمومية، ميزان المراجعة، دفتر الأستاذ العام، دفتر أستاذ الشركاء وأعمار الذمم كتقارير حية.
  • اختيار الفترة والقيود والبحث من داخل التقرير.
  • المقارنة مع الفترة السابقة أو نفس الفترة من السنة الماضية.
  • الضغط على أي حساب لفتح بنود اليومية، أو على أي سطر لفتح القيد.
  • التصدير إلى PDF أو Excel.

Correct statements

  • Balance sheet at a date: cumulative balances, whatever the start date.
  • Equity in its own section; profit not yet closed shown as Profit (Loss) to report.
  • Depreciation included in the profit and loss.
  • Report lines select accounts by account type: renaming or translating a line never changes the figures.
  • Aged balances at past dates: payments made after the date are not counted.
  • Clear Arabic accounting terms throughout.

Also included

  • Asset management and depreciation boards
  • Budgets
  • Post-dated cheques (PDC)
  • Customer follow-ups and credit limits
  • Recurring entries
  • Bank statement import (OFX, QIF, CSV, XLSX)
  • Day, bank and cash books, cash flow, tax report, journal audit
  • Lock dates and multiple invoice layouts

Works with the DoniaLink Agent Connector: the DoniaLink control app and its AI agents read the same figures your clients print.
